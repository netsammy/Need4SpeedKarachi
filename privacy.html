Need 4 Speed Karachi Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Need 4 Speed Karachi handles information when you play the game on Android, Windows, or the web. The game is published by Netsammy.

Need 4 Speed Karachi does not require account registration. Game progress, settings, unlocked cars, upgrades, challenge progress, credits, and local scores are stored on your device unless a platform service requires processing for ads, purchases, or app delivery.

Information We Collect or Process

The app may process limited gameplay and device information needed to run the game, such as app version, device type, operating system, screen size, language, control preferences, game progress, purchases restored through Google Play, ad consent status, and crash or diagnostic signals made available by platform services.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect your name, home address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, precise location, or files from your device during normal gameplay.

Local Game Data

The game stores data locally so it can provide core features, including:

  • audio, touch, tilt-steering, and gameplay settings
  • highest stage, best score, route progress, credits, garage upgrades, liveries, and selected car
  • daily and weekly challenge progress
  • purchase ownership flags after Google Play confirms a one-time product purchase or restore

Advertising

Need 4 Speed Karachi uses Google AdMob for rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device information, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and consent signals to deliver, measure, and improve ads.

You can manage ad personalization through your Android device settings, Google account settings, or consent prompts shown where required. Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only when you choose an in-game reward action such as revive or bonus rewards.

Purchases

The game uses Google Play Billing for one-time products such as premium cars, credit packs, and ad-free upgrade. Google Play processes payment information. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The app receives purchase status information from Google Play so it can unlock purchased content and restore eligible purchases.

How Information Is Used

  • to run the game, save progress, restore purchases, and keep settings consistent
  • to provide optional rewarded ads and limited interstitial advertising
  • to detect and fix crashes, compatibility problems, and performance issues
  • to comply with legal, platform, tax, billing, and policy requirements

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on the following third-party services:

  • Google Play for app distribution, purchase processing, license testing, and install services
  • Google AdMob for advertising and ad consent where applicable
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the web version and this Privacy Policy

These services process data under their own privacy policies. Please review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children's Privacy

Need 4 Speed Karachi is intended for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will review the issue.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the app, or reset browser storage for the web version. Purchase records may remain available through Google Play so purchases can be restored. You can request privacy support using the contact email below.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage, transmission, or processing is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Choices

  • Use Android settings to manage advertising ID and ad personalization.
  • Use in-game settings to control sound, music, and input preferences.
  • Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local gameplay data from your device.
  • Use Google Play purchase history and restore features for purchase-related records.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, platform requirements, advertising behavior, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.
